Noise and SNR

290 K: noise = −173.975 + 10 log₁₀(BW) + NF. The total system NF must include the RX cable, amplifier and receiver, referred to the RX antenna output; do not enter the amplifier NF alone. Tick the box to derive it with the Friis formula, treating the RX cable as a passive loss at 290 K (NF = loss).

Data Rate and Eb/N₀

Uses the total system noise figure above, at 290 K. C/N₀ = C − (−173.975 + NF); Eb/N₀ = C/N₀ − 10 log₁₀(Rb). The required Eb/N₀ depends on the modulation, coding and target bit error rate: for example, uncoded BPSK or QPSK needs about 9.6 dB for a BER of 10⁻⁵. Implementation loss covers demodulator and filter imperfections.

Measured vs. Predicted

Measure at the receiver input after the RX amplifier, under the same operating conditions. With two or more distances, a log-distance path-loss model is fitted and drawn on the chart above.

RF MOTION

Doppler Shift

Enter the non-negative line-of-sight velocity, not the total orbital velocity.

One-way, first-order approximation

Δf ≈ −f₀ × vᵣ / c
fᵣ = f₀ + Δf

c = 299,792,458 m/s; vᵣ is positive when receding. Valid for speeds far below the speed of light; the input limit is 0.01c. Two-way radar, relativistic corrections and orbit tracking are not included.
