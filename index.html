RF MOTION

Doppler Shift

Enter the non-negative line-of-sight velocity, not the total orbital velocity.

One-way, first-order approximation

Δf ≈ −f₀ × vᵣ / c
fᵣ = f₀ + Δf

c = 299,792,458 m/s; vᵣ is positive when receding. Valid for speeds far below the speed of light; the input limit is 0.01c. Two-way radar, relativistic corrections and orbit tracking are not included.
